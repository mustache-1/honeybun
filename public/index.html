Questions

Good to know

Is Honeybun free?

Yes. Honeybun is free to use.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No. You add what you spend yourself, so your bank logins stay with you.

How does Apple Pay auto-logging work?

In Settings you make a key, then set up a two-minute iPhone Shortcut automation. Every time you tap to pay, your phone sends the amount and store to Honeybun. No bank access is involved, and you can turn it off anytime.

Is there an app to download?

Honeybun works in your browser on any phone or computer. On iPhone or Android, open honeybun.me and choose Add to Home Screen to use it like an app.

Can my partner see everything?

People in your budget see shared spending, bills, and goals. Anything you mark "Only I can see this" stays private.

Can I delete my data?

Anytime. Settings lets you download a copy of your data or delete your account.

            Apple Pay auto-logging

            Every time you tap to pay, your iPhone can send the amount and store to Honeybun. No bank connection needed.

            Step 1 is making a key. It's like a password just for your Shortcut, so keep it to yourself.

            Copy this key now. For safety it's only shown once. If you lose it, make a new one.

            Set up the Shortcut (2 minutes)
            1. Open the Shortcuts app, go to Automation, tap +, and pick Transaction. Choose your card, leave it on Run Immediately, and tap Next.
            2. Tap New Blank Automation and add the action Get Contents of URL.
            3. Set the URL to , Method to POST, and under Headers add Authorization with the value Bearer followed by a space and your key.
            4. Under Request Body (JSON) add amount → the Amount from the Transaction, and store → the Merchant.
            5. Optional: add a Show Notification action with the message from the result, so Bun confirms each log.

            Honeybun picks the category from the store name and remembers how you split each store. You can edit anything after, like any other entry.
