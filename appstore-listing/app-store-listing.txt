Description (4000 max)
Honeybun is a cute little budget for you, your partner or your whole family. See what's left this month, split costs fairly, never miss a bill, and let Bun the bunny cheer you on.

NO BANK LOGIN. EVER.
Add what you earn and spend in a couple of taps. Honeybun never asks to connect to your bank.

SEE WHAT'S LEFT
One clear number for the month: what came in, what you spent and what's still due. Last month's balance carries over (or starts fresh, your choice).

BUDGET TOGETHER
Invite your partner or family with a link. Split any cost evenly, by percent or by a set amount, and see a running balance so nobody has to keep score. Share a joint account and everything adds up in one pot.

NEVER MISS A BILL
Add rent, subscriptions and paychecks once and Honeybun remembers. Get a nudge before each bill is due and see what's coming up in the next 30 days.

BUN, YOUR BUDGET BUDDY
Bun gives you friendly heads-ups when a category is close to its budget, when a subscription price goes up, and where your month is heading. Keep your hop streak going and earn carrots as you log.

PLAN AHEAD
Hop calendar of your spending, goals in honey jars, a debt payoff plan with a debt-free date, shared shopping lists and a fair-share view of who brought in what.

BUILT FOR YOUR iPHONE
Face ID lock, home-screen and lock-screen widgets, Siri shortcuts, and your data stays in sync with the web and Windows apps.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads and we never sell your data. Download or delete everything any time in Settings.

The native iPhone interface is in English. The Classic web experience also supports Spanish and Chinese.
